01 / ABOUT

Talent is everywhere. Strong technical training is not.

For eight years I taught math and computer science at a Title I high school in the Bronx, where 80% of my students were recent immigrants from 40 countries. I built the school’s CS program from scratch, chaired the math department, and brought AI into every subject I taught: rate of change in Algebra, derivatives in Calculus, chatbots in CS.

Since leaving the classroom, I’ve led curriculum, operations, and instructors at Edlyft, Coding with Kids, and AI4ALL, helping first-generation and low-income students navigate the hidden curriculum of technical interviews, team tools, and professional networks.

I bring three things that rarely come together: I know these learners, I can work with the technical content, and I already build AI-assisted curriculum and coaching workflows with a human in the loop.

CITY COLLEGE · MACHINE LEARNING · 2018

Predicting what a learner should practice next

Trained a recurrent neural network on learners’ step-by-step practice histories, using Bayesian knowledge tracing ideas to estimate mastery and recommend the next skill. Cleaned two ASSISTments datasets covering 786 and 3,898 students.

AUC of 0.85 in training and 0.81 on held-out test data

CITY COLLEGE · GRADUATE RESEARCH · 2018

Grading open responses by clustering similar answers

Compared k-means, spectral, and affinity propagation clustering for auto-grading math work, then built affinity propagation from scratch in Python and NumPy. I tested it on my own AP Calculus students’ solutions, turning each written step into a feature.

Working model that picks exemplar solutions without a preset cluster count

CITY COLLEGE · WEB-GIS · 2017

Mapping NYC schools for families

A map app that helps families compare NYC schools and districts by graduation rate, class size, safety, and nearby subways and libraries. Built with a team of 3 using JavaScript, the Google Maps API, GeoJSON, PHP, and PostGIS.

Spatial queries surface every library within 5 km of a chosen school
